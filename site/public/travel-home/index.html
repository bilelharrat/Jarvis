The problem

AI travel advice is often wrong.

In a survey of 485 US travelers who use AI to plan trips, 55% had at least one bad recommendation. Some of what they got:

27%

got wrong prices or opening hours

16%

were sent to a place that doesn’t exist

10%

were sent to a business that had closed for good

Source: Greetwell survey, August 2026, reported by Stacker. In another survey, 16.2% of travelers said they had lost money by trusting AI (Send My Bag, October 2026). Both are self-reported surveys; Eden didn’t run them.

How it will work

Plan, check, approve.

Five steps, from the first idea to the person waiting at arrivals.

  1. 01 · PLAN

    Plan together

    Tell Eden where, when and who’s coming. It drafts the days with the travel time between stops.

  2. 02 · CHECK

    Checked by EVES

    EVES (Eden’s verification and evaluation system) checks hours, closures and prices, and shows each source and when it checked.

  3. 03 · APPROVE

    You approve

    Eden’s changes show as a preview. Nothing changes until you apply them, and booked items stay locked.

  4. 04 · BOOK

    Book with a check

    Before you pay for a flight, Eden checks the fare and details again and asks you to confirm.

  5. 05 · SHARE

    Track and share

    Follow your flight on the day, and send a link to whoever is picking you up.

What Ask Eden Travel is being built to do differently.

Facts with sources

Each opening hour, price and travel time says where it came from and when it was checked. When sources disagree, you see that too.

Your plan stays yours

Every change Eden suggests is a preview you apply or skip, and you can undo it. A log shows what changed and why.

One place for the group

Plan the days together, vote on the options and split the costs, without a cap on how many expenses you add.

Flights

Checked before you pay.

These come after the planner opens. We haven’t set dates yet.

Coming later

Book flights in your plan

Before you pay, Eden checks the fare again and reads back the names, dates, airports, bags and change rules. It flags anything that clashes with your plan, like landing after your hotel’s check-in closes. Nothing is booked until you confirm.

Coming later

A link for whoever picks you up

Send a web page with your flight’s live status and about when you’ll be out at the curb. It opens in any browser, no app needed.
