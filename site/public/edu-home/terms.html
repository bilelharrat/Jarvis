Course materials terms

For professors and TAs who add materials to a course. These add to the Eden terms.

What you may upload

Only materials you have the right to share with your students for this course: your own slides, notes, lesson plans and handouts; open textbooks and openly licensed works (for example Creative Commons, OpenStax); and readings your school licenses for this use. Publisher textbooks usually can’t be uploaded, even if your students bought them.

How we use them

We keep the text of your materials so Eden can answer your students’ questions from them and cite the page or slide. We don’t use them to train AI models, and we don’t show them to anyone outside your course. They’re deleted when you remove them or delete the course.

Takedown requests

If you believe material in a course infringes your copyright, email support@askeden.com with: the work, where it appears (the course name or join code and the file name), your contact details, and a statement that you’re the rights holder or authorized to act for them. We’ll remove infringing material promptly and tell the professor. Professors who repeatedly upload infringing material lose access.

Accuracy

Eden checks that each cited quote appears in its source, but it can still make mistakes. It’s a study aid, not a replacement for your teaching or for students’ own work.
