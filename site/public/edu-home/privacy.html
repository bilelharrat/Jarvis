Student privacy notice

How Eden for Education handles what students and professors share. This adds to the Eden privacy policy, which also applies.

Who can use it

Eden for Education is for people 13 and older. When you join a class you confirm your age. If we learn that someone under 13 has joined, we delete their membership and study chats.

What your professor sees

  • The class list: the name you typed when you joined, and when you joined.
  • Insights, without names: which slides and pages the class asks about most, practice quiz averages, and questions the materials didn’t cover. A question that wasn’t covered shows only after at least three different students asked it, and never with who asked.
  • If your professor funds a course budget: how much of it the class used each day, and the top users by amount. These are anonymous by default; the professor can choose to show the names you gave when you joined. Never your questions.

Your professor and TAs can’t read your study chats.

What we keep

  • Your notebook (notes you write and answers you clip), so it follows you between devices. Only you can open it; it’s deleted when you delete a note, leave the class, or the course is deleted.
  • Your study chats, so they follow you between devices. Only you can open them. They’re deleted when you delete them, leave the class, are removed from it, or the course is deleted.
  • Course materials your professor added, as text, so Eden can answer from them.
  • Anonymous records for insights (topics, counts), kept for up to a year and deleted with the course.

Paying for your study AI

Draft billing notice, marked for the owner’s legal review. Your course questions are free within a daily allowance for your account (across all your courses, refilled at midnight UTC), or paid from a course budget your professor funds. When both are used up, Eden stops and asks; it uses your own Eden allowance (your plan, trial or credits) only if you choose that. To keep the free allowance fair we count, per day, how much each account used and a one-way code made from the network address studying (never the address itself), and use these counts only to apply the daily limits; each new day’s counts replace the last.

What we never do

  • We don’t use students’ chats or course materials to train AI models, ours or anyone else’s.
  • We don’t sell student information or use it for advertising.
  • We don’t share it except with the AI providers that answer your questions (OpenAI, Google, Moonshot, or Anthropic on your own key), under terms that don’t let them train on it, and as the law requires.

Schools

When a school or district uses Eden for Education under an agreement with us, we act for the school as the law (including FERPA in the US) expects, and the school’s agreement takes precedence over this notice where they differ.

Your choices

You can delete any study chat, leave a class at any time, or delete your Eden account, which deletes everything above. Questions or requests: support@askeden.com.
