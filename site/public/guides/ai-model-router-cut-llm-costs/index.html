How query-level routing makes a $10/month subscription possible

In a typical workday, a power user’s prompts span four distinct complexity tiers:

  • Simple (~30% of prompts): Quick rewrites, formatting, syntax lookups, and short translations. Flagship reasoning tokens are wasted here.
  • Moderate (~35% of prompts): Drafting emails, summarizing documents, standard SQL or React components.
  • Complex (~25% of prompts): Multi-file debugging, financial modeling, comparative research with citations.
  • Expert (~10% of prompts): Formal proofs, concurrency race conditions, compiler design, and high-stakes factual synthesis.
