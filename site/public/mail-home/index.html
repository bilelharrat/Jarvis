01 · What it does

Less reading, fewer surprises.

Four things Eden does in your mail, and the one thing it never does on its own.

01 · SORT

It says why

What needs you comes first, and each email says why: a deadline, a question, a lookalike address. The rest folds into bundles.

02 · ASK

Answers with sources

Ask about one email or your whole inbox. Each answer shows the emails it used, the model and how long it took.

03 · DRAFT

Replies in your style

Eden drafts from the way you already write, with the thread in mind. Edit anything, or ask for it shorter or friendlier.

04 · SEND

You press Send

Before anything goes, Eden shows who it goes to and what it checked: dates, names, amounts, anyone new to the thread. Undo for ten seconds after.
