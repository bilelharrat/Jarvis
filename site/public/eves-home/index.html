Factual answers made without a search are checked against the web afterwards, and corrected when the web disagrees.

What the numbers can’t tell you.

EVES declines more often

It said it didn’t know on 20.6% of the questions, against about 1% for the Gemini models. Part of its lead comes from that: both Gemini models got more questions right than EVES did.

Web search is a big part

The three models answered from memory, by design. About 60% of EVES’s gain over Claude Sonnet 5.5 came from using the web. A model with web search of its own wasn’t part of this comparison, so this doesn’t show that EVES beats one.

One run, one date

Each model answered each question once, on 9 October 2026, with one set-up of EVES, without OpenAI’s models. With them, EVES could pick different models and the numbers could change.

Grading isn’t perfect

An automatic grader and an AI judge graded the answers, and both make mistakes. The judge is also one of the models compared. The questions are a sample, and short factual questions are only one kind of question.
