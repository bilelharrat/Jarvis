01 · JUST TALK

Say “Eden”

Say "Eden" from across the room, or press ⌥ Space on a Mac or Ctrl+Alt+J on Windows. It answers out loud and lets you talk over it.

02 · YOUR DAY

Handled

Calendar, mail, reminders and a morning brief (messages too, on a Mac), with a card to confirm anything that sends or pays.

03 · EDEN CODE

Included

Click Code in the dock, or say "Eden Secretary, let's code": Eden Code opens on the same sessions.

It asks before anything that sends or pays, and Eden Code comes with it.
