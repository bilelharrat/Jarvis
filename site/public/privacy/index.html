Privacy Policy

Effective October 7, 2026

Eden (askeden.com, the Eden apps and J.A.R.V.I.S.) is run by Harrat Global Holdings, Inc. (“we”, “us”). This policy says, in plain English, what we keep, what we pass on and why. Questions: support@askeden.com.

The short version

  • We don’t sell your data, and there are no ads.
  • Your chats are sent to the AI company whose model answers them, and askeden.com does not keep a copy for itself. To show your chat history on your other devices, we store it encrypted on askeden.com; deleting a chat, or your account, erases it there. Temporary chats are never stored. You can switch to end-to-end encryption, where we can’t read your chats at all.
  • If you import your ChatGPT history, the export file is processed in your browser, never uploaded, and the chats are stored like your other chats.
  • We keep what’s needed to run your account: who you are when you sign in, your devices, your plan and what you’ve used.

What we keep

  • Your account: a random account id, when it was made, your plan (trial or Plus), and how much of the included AI you’ve used.
  • How you sign in: for Apple and Google, a one-way hash of the id they give us (never the raw id); for Google, the email address it shares. For a passkey, its public key and signature counter (never anything secret). Apple’s sign-in shares no email with us.
  • Your devices and browsers: each one’s name (like “Safari on a Mac”), app version, and when it was added and last used. A browser stays signed in for at most 30 days.
  • Your own API keys, if you add any (OpenAI, Google Gemini, Moonshot, Anthropic): encrypted, used only to call that provider for you, never shown back in full.
  • Chat history, to show it on every device where you sign in: your conversations are stored encrypted on askeden.com (images stay on the device they were added on). If you turn on end-to-end encryption, they are encrypted on your device with a key we don’t have, and we store data we can’t read.
  • Things you choose to make: pages you publish, background tasks and saved workflows, team spaces and delegates. Previews of generated pages (“artifacts”) are kept for a few hours, then deleted.
  • Gmail and Google Calendar, only if you connect them (see below): the access Google grants, encrypted.
  • Operational records: rate-limit counters and short error logs that help us keep the service working. We don’t log the contents of your chats.

Who we pass data to, and why

  • AI model providers: the messages, files and images in a conversation go to the provider of the model that answers: OpenAI, Google (Gemini) or Moonshot AI (Kimi) on the AI included with your account (Help’s chat, @Eden in Eden Messenger, background tasks and the J.A.R.V.I.S. apps’ included AI too), and Anthropic (Claude) only when you’ve added your own Anthropic API key. To pick a model, Eden may ask Google’s Gemini to rate how hard a message is. Each provider handles that data under its own API terms. Videos you attach are sent to Google (Gemini) for processing and are not stored by Eden; Eden deletes them at Google after the reply, and Google deletes any left within 48 hours.
  • Cloudflare hosts askeden.com and runs it (including speech-to-text for dictation in browsers that can’t do it themselves).
  • Stripe processes payments for Plus on the web. We never see your full card number. Plus bought in the iPhone app goes through Apple.
  • Apple and Google when you sign in with them, and Apple for app notifications.
  • Fish Audio turns text into the JARVIS voice, if you use read-aloud with that voice.
  • Your own Mac, if you link one: requests for things that live on it (Jarvis, Code mode, local models) are passed to it.
  • Connected apps you approve, such as Eden Messenger: they can only ask Eden what you choose to send them, and you can revoke them any time.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect people and the service from fraud and abuse.

Gmail and Google Calendar

Eden reads your Gmail or Google Calendar only after you connect them, and only to do what you ask: show and search your mail, draft, send or schedule a message, read and add events. You can disconnect them in Eden, which also revokes Eden’s access at Google.

Eden’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular, we use Google user data only to provide the features you use in Eden; we don’t use it for advertising, don’t sell it, and don’t let people read it unless you ask us to (for example, for support), it’s needed for security or the law requires it. When you ask Eden about your mail or calendar, the parts it needs are sent to the AI model that answers, for that answer only. Google user data is not used to train AI models.

Memory

On askeden.com, Eden can remember helpful details across your chats, such as your name, preferences and ongoing projects. It saves them when you ask (“remember that…”) or when a small Google Gemini model, run on your latest message after a reply, finds a lasting detail worth keeping; it skips sensitive topics such as health, religion, sexuality, politics, precise location, account numbers and passwords unless you ask it to remember them. Saved memories are stored encrypted with your account, added to your later chats so the answering model can use them, and never shared with delegates or team spaces. Temporary chats neither use nor add memories. You can see and delete them, delete them all, or turn memory off in Settings › Memory, or say “forget that…”; deleting your account deletes them.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data while your account exists. Browser sessions end after 30 days; artifacts after a few hours; sign-in and payment attempts after minutes. When you delete your account, every device and browser is signed out at once, and everything stored with it is deleted: your account data, your ways to sign in (Apple, Google) and passkeys, and the pages you published (their links stop working). Connected Google access is revoked and an active web subscription is cancelled; a subscription bought in the App Store is cancelled in your Apple account’s settings. Payment records Stripe and Apple must keep for tax and accounting stay with them.

Your choices

  • See and change: your account page in Eden shows your plan, devices, ways to sign in and connected apps. You can remove a sign-in method, sign browsers out, revoke apps and disconnect Google there.
  • Delete your account: on askeden.com (your account page › Delete account, then type DELETE), in the J.A.R.V.I.S. app (Settings › Account › Delete Account), or email support@askeden.com from an address on your account and we’ll delete it for you.
  • Ask us: for a copy of your data, a correction, or anything else about your privacy, email us. We answer within 30 days.

Security

Everything travels over HTTPS. Session cookies can’t be read by scripts. API keys and Google access are encrypted at rest; synced chats are encrypted at rest (or end-to-end, if you choose that). No system is perfect: if something goes wrong that affects you, we’ll tell you.

Children

Eden isn’t meant for children under 13 (or the minimum age where you live), and we don’t knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has an account, email us and we’ll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top, and tell you in Eden before a significant change takes effect.
