A few things worth knowing

Good questions.

What is Eden for Business?+

Eden for Business brings together Ask Eden and the Eden family of products in one place, helping you find tools for everyday work, software development, education, and AI infrastructure.

Do I need the whole product family?+

No. Start with the product that fits the work you have today. This website is a guide to the family, not a separate all-in-one subscription. Pricing and availability are on each product’s website.

Where should I start?+

For everyday questions, writing, and research, start with Ask Eden. If you’re building software, try Eden Code. For teaching and studying with your own materials, explore Eden for Education.

Can I use Eden in an existing AI workflow?+

Explore Eden Router for routing and API integration, and EVES for factual verification. See those product pages for current access, integration requirements, and limitations.
