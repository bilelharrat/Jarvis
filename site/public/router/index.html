Eden Router v2

Faster, sharper on expert tasks, and built against hallucinations

Eden Router v2 re-architects the task analyzer, the Gemini 3.6 Flash classifier, the factual reliability engine, and the developer API.

100% complexity accuracy

0% → 100% on expert tasks

v1’s linear keyword sum capped single-domain prompts below the expert threshold (0.68), misclassifying 100% of terse expert math, systems, and compiler tasks. v2 adds structural complexity signals, domain interaction bonuses, and calibrated boundaries (0.22 / 0.48 / 0.72), reaching 100% accuracy across simple, moderate, complex, and expert benchmarks.

Hallucination mitigation first

Factual reliability (0–100)

Every (model, effort) pair carries a factual reliability score calibrated against our 432-question SimpleQA-Verified + MHFPQ evaluation and AA-Omniscience knowledge z-scores. When riskAware is on (default), high-risk medical, legal, financial, and citation queries automatically shift toward higher-reliability models and step up reasoning effort.

Sub-50 µs hot path

6.2 µs warm · 40 µs cold

Prefix-indexed phrase tables, pre-compiled regexes, and a 64-entry LRU hot-path cache route ~160,000 cached prompts/sec (~25,000 cold prompts/sec) with zero network calls. When Gemini 3.6 Flash classification is enabled, a 256-entry LRU cache and in-flight request coalescing eliminate duplicate ratings.

Production orchestration

Failover, cascades & batch budgets

Built-in cross-provider failoverChain for high availability, 2-stage speculative planCascade (fast draft + frontier verifier, automatically bypassed when hallucination risk is high), and greedy marginal-utility routeBatch under a strict maxTotalCostUSD cap.

How we measured

Graded tasks, keys we computed ourselves

The test

  • 44 tasks: 9 simple, 12 moderate, 13 complex, 10 expert. 38 graded by rule, 6 by an AI judge with fixed rubrics.
  • Every answer key is computed and cross-checked by a second, independent method.
  • 18 models from Anthropic, OpenAI, Google and Moonshot, each at the effort it was measured at.
  • The router ran on rules alone and made no model calls of its own.

Limits

  • Small sample. 44 tasks, one run each. Gaps of a few points between levels are within noise.
  • Our tasks, not yours. They lean toward exact computation and code.
  • Prices and models move. These are October 2026 prices and versions.
  • One judge is also a contestant. The AI judge for six tasks is one of the models compared.

The Eden Router v2 APIprivate beta

One OpenAI-compatible endpoint plus dedicated routing, failover, cascade and batch endpoints. Send a chat request with your own provider keys; Eden Router v2 picks the model and effort among the providers you sent a key for, weighs factual reliability on high-risk prompts, calls the provider, and returns the answer with full x_eden telemetry.

Invite only for now: one key per client, made by us. Server to server only (no browser calls). We keep your key hashed (SHA-256) and log numbers only (time, model, tokens, cost, latency, status), never prompts, answers or provider keys.

curl https://edenrouter.com/v1/chat/completions \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $EDEN_ROUTER_KEY" \
  -H "x-openai-key: $OPENAI_API_KEY" \
  -H "x-gemini-key: $GEMINI_API_KEY" \
  -H "content-type: application/json" \
  -d '{"model": "eden-router:level-3",
       "x_eden": {"risk_aware": true, "classifier": "auto"},
       "messages": [{"role": "user", "content": "Refactor this to fix a race condition"}]}'
from openai import OpenAI

client = OpenAI(base_url="https://edenrouter.com/v1", api_key=EDEN_ROUTER_KEY,
                default_headers={"x-anthropic-key": ANTHROPIC_API_KEY, "x-gemini-key": GEMINI_API_KEY})
r = client.chat.completions.create(model="eden-router", messages=[{"role": "user", "content": "Hi"}],
                                   extra_body={"x_eden": {"efficiency": 70, "performance": 60, "risk_aware": True}})
print(r.choices[0].message.content, r.model, r.x_eden["reliability"], r.x_eden["actual_cost_usd"])

Models: eden-router (balanced) or eden-router:level-1 (max efficiency) to level-5 (max performance). Optional x_eden: efficiency and performance (0–100), providers (openai, anthropic, gemini, moonshot), classifier (off, auto, always: a Gemini 3.6 Flash rating on your Gemini key, about $0.0005), risk_aware (boolean, default true: shifts high-risk factual queries toward higher-reliability models and steps up effort). Provider keys: x-openai-key, x-anthropic-key, x-gemini-key, x-moonshot-key. Streaming ("stream": true) works as in OpenAI's API. Every answer carries x_eden: the model, effort, factual reliability (0–100), risk assessment, estimated and actual cost, and the rationale. On a provider error before any text, it tries the router's next pick once.

Routing & orchestration endpoints (no provider call): POST /v1/route (single decision with reliability & risk), POST /v1/route/failover (provider-diverse failover chain), POST /v1/route/cascade (2-stage speculative cascade with high-risk safeguard), POST /v1/route/batch (budget-constrained batch optimization under max_total_cost_usd), GET /v1/models, and GET /v1/usage (your requests and daily totals). Limits per key: requests a minute and a daily spend cap; past them you get a 429.
