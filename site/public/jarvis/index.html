Your Mac,

J.A.R.V.I.S. is a voice assistant for your Mac. Say what you need, and it handles it: your calendar and mail, messages, the web, your files, your Mac itself, and your code.

Download for Mac Version 0.1.0 · 296 MB · Apple silicon · macOS 14 or later

What it does

Talk, type, or wave. It takes it from there.

Just talk

Say "Jarvis" from across the room, or press ⌥ Space. It listens, answers out loud, and lets you talk over it.

Your day, handled

Calendar, mail, messages, reminders and a morning brief, with a card to confirm anything that sends or pays.

Jarvis Code

A full coding agent for your projects: plans, edits, reviews, checkpoints you can rewind, and steering while it works.

A browser it can drive

Tabs, an ad blocker, and an assistant that reads pages, fills forms and gets through sites for you.

Hands-free control

Point, pinch and scroll with your hand through the camera, or control your whole Mac by voice.

Private by design

It runs on your Mac: its memory and file index stay there, only what a request needs goes to Claude, and your key lives in your Keychain.

Install in a minute

Signed and notarized by Apple. It updates itself from then on.
