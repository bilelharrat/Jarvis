Accuracy

Fewer made-up answers.

We asked EVES, our fact-checker, and three leading AI models the same 432 factual questions. EVES made something up the least often.

How often each one made something up on 432 factual questions, lower is better: EVES 9.7% (95% interval 7.3% to 12.9%), Gemini 3.1 Pro (preview) 18.3% (14.9% to 22.2%), Gemini 3.8 Flash 21.8% (18.1% to 25.9%), Claude Sonnet 5.5 24.5% (20.7% to 28.8%). EVES checks the web when needed and says when it isn’t sure; the models answered directly from memory. October 2026.
