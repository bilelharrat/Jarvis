Jarvis, six ways

Six directions for the main window. Each one shows the same moment, Friday 8:42 AM, just after Jarvis has answered “what’s on today, and how are the markets looking?”, so you can compare them like for like. Click one to open it full size.

New directions

Static, high-fidelity mockups. Nothing is wired up, and all the data is made up.

01

Morning Edition

Grows out of today’s Ambient Orb into a newspaper front page for your day. The reply is the lead story, the agenda and markets run in columns, and the orb becomes a small blue seal.

LightEditorialCalm
02

Mission Control

A dark bento dashboard where everything is visible at once: voice, agenda timeline, markets, Jarvis Code, activity and system. Amber is the only warm colour, and it only marks things that need you.

DarkDenseAt a glance
03

Halo

Voice first, and almost nothing else. The orb becomes a thin ring of light, the reply is set large, and a single card appears when Jarvis needs you. A strip along the bottom shows the four voice states.

DarkMinimalVoice states
04

Workspace

A native three-pane Mac app built around the conversation, with past chats and Jarvis Code on the left and rich cards in the thread. A Today inspector on the right holds the context.

LightNative macOSKeyboard + voice
05

Workshop

The cinematic one: a radial hologram in hot-rod red and Stark gold. Panels orbit the arc reactor on leader lines, and the heads-up runs around the outer ring.

DarkCinematicRadial
06

Island

Jarvis with no window at all. A black capsule hangs under the menu bar and opens up to answer, approvals and heads-ups arrive as Mac notifications, and Jarvis Code runs as a small floating pill.
