UNNotificationAction

A task your app performs in response to a notification that the system delivers.

iOS 10.0+iPadOS 10.0+macOS 10.14+watchOS 3.0+
Declaration
class UNNotificationAction : NSObject
Overview

Use UNNotificationAction objects to define the actions people can take from a notification’s interface without opening your app.

Group related actions into a category, then register the category when your app launches. The system shows the buttons when a notification of that category arrives.

Each action has a unique identifier, a title shown on the button, and options such as requiring the device to be unlocked.
